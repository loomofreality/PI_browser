PI Browser

Privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

PI Browser is a personal, non-commercial project. This policy covers the PI Browser app for iPhone and iPad and the website at portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not collect, receive, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your bookmarks, the last 100 pages you visited, and your reading preferences (such as text size and language) on your device. The website keeps your reading preferences in your browser’s storage. None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app or clear the website’s data in your browser, though your device’s own backups may include it.

When the app goes online

The complete collection is installed with the app and works without a connection. The app only goes online when you ask it to:

  • Check website release, on the Collection screen, downloads a small file from the website.
  • External references, such as bibliography or comparison links, open in Safari inside the app.

As with any web request, those sites see technical details such as your IP address. They handle them under their own privacy policies.

Website hosting

The website and the volume PDFs are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits for security and operational purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Children

We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
