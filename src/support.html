Partial Inventory support

For questions, corrections, or problems with the app or the website, contact loomofreality@gmail.com. If you are reporting a problem, please include your device, the app’s website release (shown on the Collection screen), and a link to the page. The app’s Share button copies a link that opens on the public website.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Search, every text, subjects, study guides and all volume PDFs are installed with the app.

How do new texts arrive?

Through app updates from the App Store. Updates keep your bookmarks.

Where are my bookmarks stored?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Are these authenticated texts?

Most translations on this site are not authenticated. Those texts which are authenticated are indicated everywhere in blue type. This is a personal project, and accuracy is not guaranteed. For authenticated texts, visit the Bahá’í Reference Library at bahai.org/library.
