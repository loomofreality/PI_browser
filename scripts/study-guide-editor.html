Edit a study guide

Change a guide’s title, introduction, reading arc, reading order and notes, which sentences of each reading are shown, which readings are left out, discussion questions, neighbor invitations and comparative reading. Save keeps a backup on this computer. “Propose saved guides” opens a pull request on GitHub with a summary of the changes; merging it publishes them.

The wording of a quotation is never changed: a reading can only be shortened to some of its whole sentences, or left out. Readings with an authorized translation keep at least one authorized sentence and cannot be left out. The introduction and questions appear only in the study guide’s PDF view.
